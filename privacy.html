Privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. The controller is the provider named in the legal notice.

In short: Keeperling is a children's game that collects no data. No ads, no analytics or tracking, no accounts, no microphone, no internet connection required.

The Keeperling app

Keeperling does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It runs fully offline with no ads, no analytics or crash services, no account and no social features. Game progress and settings are stored only on the device and are removed on uninstall. The app does not access the microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location. It contains no outbound links reachable by children. The app is designed for children (Apple Kids category); we collect no data from children and share none with third parties.

There are no in-app purchases. Purchase and installation are handled solely by the respective store, the Microsoft Store (Windows) or the Apple App Store (iPad), under Microsoft's or Apple's privacy statements. At most we receive aggregated sales and crash statistics without names.
