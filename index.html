16 animals, 6 areas

Savanna, rainforest, mountain forest, coast, Australia and homeland: which habitat suits which animal?

Feed them right

Meat, hay, leaves or fish? The animals kindly show you what they like.

Observe

Dust baths, grooming, naps: careful watching fills up the animal encyclopedia.

Checked animal facts

All facts are carefully checked and written for children.

Language learning through play

  • Read-aloud with follow-along highlighting: the spoken text is highlighted as it is read out.
  • Word cards: new words are collected as cards in the encyclopedia.
  • Syllable help: long words can be split into syllables.
  • Second language: texts and words are also available in a second language.

Screenshots

For parents

  • No ads, no in-game purchases, no timers.
  • No data collection, no tracking, no microphone, no accounts.
  • Works fully offline.
  • Explorer mode (ages 5 to 7) with big symbols, Researcher mode (ages 8 to 10) with short texts.
